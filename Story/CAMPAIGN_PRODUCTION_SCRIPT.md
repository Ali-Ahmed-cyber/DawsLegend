# DAWS LEGEND — Campaign Production Script

## Campaign promise
A 30-mission Sudanese open-city action story set in fictional Al-Mirfa, inspired by the atmosphere of Omdurman, Khartoum and Bahri. The hero wears the white Real Madrid shirt; Tawfiq wears yellow; Amjad wears black. Their investigation begins with missing clinic supplies and grows into a riverfront land-grab conspiracy. The tone blends suspense, vehicle pursuits, market bustle, tea stalls, minibus routes, workshops, neighborhood football and Sudanese humor. Characters, missions and locations are original.

## The connected mystery
The Broker poses as a public benefactor. Logistics manager Muntasir changes manifests; Abu Sahl organizes intimidation and false roadblocks; The Needle leaks investigation details through the relay. They create shortages to buy businesses cheaply and seize riverfront property. The team must prove the case through a sequence of seals, ledgers, a convoy recording, radio frequencies, worker testimony, ferry contracts and a missing ledger page.

## Act I — The Neighborhood Knows
1. **A Radio Before Sunrise** — `chapter01_radio`: meet Tawfiq and Amjad at Mariam's tea stall and speak to Hiba about a missing clinic shipment.
2. **Crates With No Seal** — `chapter01_plaza`: inspect empty crates; Amjad spots careful relabeling.
3. **Bashir's Back Gate** — `chapter01_workshop`: meet mechanic Bashir and protect his workshop ledger.
4. **Three Bundles, One Lie** — `chapter01_supplies`: recover three supply bundles with inconsistent tags.
5. **The Driver Who Ran** — `chapter01_driver`: survive a souq ambush and recover Sami's delivery token.

## Act II — Smoke in the Souq
6. **Smoke Over the Market** — `chapter02_market`: search a burned storage shop for a scorched manifest.
7. **The Nurse's Stamp** — `chapter02_nurse`: Salwa proves the medical seals were swapped after the depot.
8. **A Minibus Full of Rumors** — `chapter02_minibus`: follow Idris through three witness stops and learn of a river-road detour.
9. **The Night Convoy** — `chapter02_convoy`: retrieve a dash recorder from the lay-by before the crew returns.
10. **Sami's Second Chance** — `chapter02_sami`: protect Sami, who drove under threats, and hear his lead about Muntasir.

## Act III — Friends Under Pressure
11. **A Favor for Tawfiq** — `chapter03_tawfiq`: Tawfiq's shortcut becomes a pursuit through a construction lane; the network learns the team's route.
12. **Amjad's Missing Hours** — `chapter03_amjad`: confront Amjad about a delivery job he hid out of shame, not loyalty.
13. **The Clinic at Dusk** — `chapter03_clinic`: defend the clinic supply route while Salwa and Fawzi move families to safety.
14. **The Needle in the Static** — `chapter03_needle`: find a rooftop jammer and recover coded radio frequencies.
15. **No One Leaves Bashir Behind** — `chapter03_bashir`: rescue Bashir and learn a page was torn from the original ledger.

## Act IV — The Riverfront Deal
16. **The Silent Relay** — `chapter04_relay`: meet the Technician and restore the jammed district relay.
17. **Keep the Technician Moving** — `chapter04_escort`: escort the Technician to Hiba's broadcast room; wait for him to catch up.
18. **A Voice Without a Name** — `chapter04_broadcast`: get to the studio so Hiba can broadcast verified facts without exposing witnesses.
19. **Paper on the Water** — `chapter04_ferry`: retrieve a waterproof case of suspicious riverfront property contracts.
20. **The Broker's Charity** — `chapter04_charity`: identify Muntasir's handoff at a public food distribution before a staged raid scatters the crowd.

## Act V — The City Turns
21. **A City of Roadblocks** — `chapter05_roadblocks`: navigate fake closures and reopen a safe route for clinic supplies.
22. **Free the Depot Workers** — `chapter05_workers`: defeat the guards holding workers who refused to falsify cargo counts.
23. **Tawfiq's Debt** — `chapter05_tawfiq_debt`: find Tawfiq after a risky bargain goes wrong; he admits the shortcut helped the enemy track the team.
24. **The Ledger Has Two Pages** — `chapter05_ledger`: recover the missing ledger page and reveal an insider altered official records.
25. **Amjad Tells the Truth** — `chapter05_amjad_truth`: Amjad hands over his old vehicle logbook and identifies the drop vehicle.

## Act VI — Dawn Belongs to Everyone
26. **Road to the River** — `chapter06_river_road`: clear the final route so the clinic truck can pass.
27. **The Witness Protection Run** — `chapter06_witness_run`: move Sami and the Survivor between safehouses without losing their evidence.
28. **Hold the Central Depot** — `chapter06_depot`: hold the evidence room while the neighborhood brings water and bandages.
29. **The Man Behind the Manifest** — `chapter06_muntasir`: confront Muntasir and connect the records to the Broker's planned river escape.
30. **Dawn of Al-Mirfa** — `chapter06_dawn`: defeat the Broker's final enforcer, confront the Broker and complete Hiba's verified broadcast. Saving trapped workers matters more than a reckless chase. The final scene returns to the clinic and tea stall: the city is not magically fixed, but the neighborhood has the truth and a chance to rebuild.

## Optional side stories
- **Tea Before Trouble:** retrieve Mariam's tea cart and unlock a safe contact.
- **The Lost Football:** search with neighborhood kids and unlock friendship dialogue.
- **Last Minibus Home:** help Idris repair a minibus and open a faster route.
- **Auntie's Medicine:** deliver a clinic package without combat.
- **The Workshop Bet:** recover a rare spare part for Bashir.
- **Market Day:** expose a decoy crew targeting market stalls.
- **The Old Photograph:** return a family photo to its owner.
- **A Radio for the Block:** gather parts for Hiba's community radio point.
- **The Missing Water Tank:** uncover an Abu Sahl property scam.
- **One More Match:** play football with Tawfiq and Amjad; dialogue changes after Act V.

## Release gates
The runtime contains 30 mission definitions and matching markers, but source audits do not prove an Unreal compile or playable release. Compile in UE 5.4, fix compiler/runtime errors, test all 30 missions including death/restore, pickup progress, escort distance, vehicle entry/exit, final enforcer and campaign restart. Final likeness photos, proper animation rigs, polished assets, audio, lighting and packaging remain future production work. The three main likenesses are added last and must never be replaced by unrelated people.

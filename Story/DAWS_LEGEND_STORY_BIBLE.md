# DAWS LEGEND — Story Bible: Sudan, One City, Many Secrets

## The identity that must never change
- The playable hero is the user, whose final likeness is integrated at the end: white Real Madrid shirt.
- **Tawfiq** is the closest friend in yellow.
- **Amjad** is the closest friend in black.
- These are the three real main-character identities. Never replace them with unrelated people.
- Every other person is an original fictional character.

## The city: Al-Mirfa
The story takes place in **Al-Mirfa**, a fictional Sudanese city drawing atmosphere from Omdurman, Khartoum and Bahri without pretending to be a real map. The Nile bends around old neighborhoods; the souq wakes before sunrise; tea ladies run small roadside spots; minibuses, rickshaws, buses and battered pickups fight for space; football talk carries across courtyards; workshops and spare-parts alleys never sleep. Sand, heat, power cuts, radio chatter, generator hum, wedding drums, prayer-time street rhythms, and sudden summer rain all shape the city's mood. Sudanese Arabic dialogue, humor, hospitality and neighborhood loyalty are central to the story.

## Genre and tone
An original third-person open-city action crime drama: freedom to explore, drive, meet people, discover side stories, and choose how to approach missions; tense chases, ambushes, betrayals, rescues, mysteries and set pieces; but with Sudanese humor, friendship and community at the heart. The inspiration is the broad freedom and cinematic pacing of open-world crime games, not copied characters, dialogue, missions, logos, maps or assets from GTA or any other franchise.

## Main premise
During a period of rising prices, blackouts and interrupted supply routes, a chain of medical and food shipments disappears. At first it looks like ordinary theft. Hiba, a young community radio presenter, receives a recording of a driver asking why his manifest has been changed. The hero, Tawfiq and Amjad help a neighborhood clinic recover one shipment. They discover that a network is using fake delivery orders, staged road closures, forged seals, and paid informants to redirect supplies. The stolen goods are only one side of the operation: the network is buying land, silencing witnesses and manipulating transport routes to gain control of Al-Mirfa's riverfront.

## The hidden truth
The Broker is a public-facing businessman who funds community events and presents himself as a savior. He is not the mastermind alone. His logistics manager **Muntasir** has been quietly changing records; a respected fixer **Abu Sahl** connects him to corrupt officials and private enforcers; and a radio technician known as **The Needle** sells information about who is investigating. They plan to trigger a false shortage, buy desperate businesses cheaply, and seize the riverfront depot through forged contracts. The plot must be uncovered through physical evidence, witness testimony, audio tapes, vehicle logs and conflicting accounts—not a single convenient confession.

## The friends' emotional arc
- **Hero:** starts by helping his own neighborhood and becomes the person willing to carry evidence when everyone else is frightened.
- **Tawfiq (yellow):** quick, bold and funny; acts before thinking, but never abandons a friend. His risky shortcut causes a major setback, then he earns back trust through a rescue.
- **Amjad (black):** observant, guarded and good with routes and mechanics. He knows more about the early convoy movements than he admits because he once did a delivery job for Muntasir. He hid it out of shame, not because he joined the network.
- The friendship is tested by money, suspicion and danger. No cheap betrayal: every disagreement grows from earlier events and is resolved through action.

## Core cast
- **Hiba:** radio presenter and evidence organizer; sharp, brave, refuses to broadcast unverified claims.
- **Bashir:** mechanic and vehicle contact; dry humor, knows every workshop and back road.
- **Captain Nadir:** investigator who insists evidence be admissible and witnesses protected.
- **Fawzi:** shopkeeper who coordinates food, water and shelter for residents.
- **Mariam:** tea-stall owner who hears neighborhood gossip and knows who visits which office.
- **Sami the Courier:** young driver trapped in the operation; first appears guilty, later becomes a key witness.
- **The Technician:** repairs the silent relay and must be escorted.
- **Salwa:** clinic nurse who can identify altered medical seals.
- **Idris:** minibus driver who knows the city routes and can assist during pursuits.
- **The Survivor:** witness from a night convoy, traumatized but observant.
- **The Worker:** depot employee who refused to falsify a cargo count.
- **Muntasir:** the Broker's logistics manager; calm, organized and dangerous because he controls the paperwork.
- **Abu Sahl:** fixer who arranges intimidation and false road closures.
- **The Needle:** radio technician selling information to both sides.
- **The Broker:** public benefactor and visible face of the network.

## Narrative rules
1. Each major reveal must be foreshadowed at least one mission earlier.
2. A clue found in one chapter must change a later mission, dialogue line or route.
3. Give every antagonist a practical motive; avoid cartoon villains.
4. Use Sudanese expressions naturally, with humor in safe moments and restraint in traumatic scenes.
5. Keep civilians human; no mission treats the city or its people as disposable.
6. Optional side activities deepen character and unlock supplies, safehouses, routes or witness trust, but the main plot remains finishable.
7. Main-character photo likenesses remain the final art stage; story continuity must not depend on those photos.

## Campaign structure: 30 main missions across 6 acts

### Act I — The Neighborhood Knows (Missions 1–5)
1. **A Radio Before Sunrise:** meet Tawfiq and Amjad at the neighborhood tea stall; Hiba reports a missing medical shipment.
2. **Crates With No Seal:** secure the plaza and inspect the first empty crates.
3. **Bashir's Back Gate:** help Bashir recover a workshop ledger before hired men burn it.
4. **Three Bundles, One Lie:** collect three hidden supply bundles and discover that the manifest lists a fourth.
5. **The Driver Who Ran:** chase Sami's pickup through the souq; he escapes, leaving a delivery token.

### Act II — Smoke in the Souq (Missions 6–10)
6. **Smoke Over the Market:** search a burned store while avoiding a second ambush.
7. **The Nurse's Stamp:** Salwa proves the medical seals were swapped at the clinic.
8. **A Minibus Full of Rumors:** ride with Idris and question three witnesses along the route.
9. **The Night Convoy:** follow tire tracks and recover a dash recorder before the crew returns.
10. **Sami's Second Chance:** corner Sami; choose to protect him from the network instead of handing him to the wrong fixer. His testimony points to Muntasir.

### Act III — Friends Under Pressure (Missions 11–15)
11. **A Favor for Tawfiq:** Tawfiq borrows a vehicle for a shortcut; the shortcut turns into a chase through a construction lane.
12. **Amjad's Missing Hours:** confront Amjad about his old delivery job and compare his route notes with the manifest.
13. **The Clinic at Dusk:** defend the clinic and evacuate medicine before the power fails.
14. **The Needle in the Static:** track a radio jammer to a rooftop relay and recover a coded frequency list.
15. **No One Leaves Bashir Behind:** rescue Bashir after he is trapped in his workshop; learn the Broker's men are looking for the original ledger.

### Act IV — The Riverfront Deal (Missions 16–20)
16. **The Silent Relay:** repair the district relay with the Technician while hostile scouts close in.
17. **Keep the Technician Moving:** escort the Technician to Hiba's broadcast room; if the route is unsafe, take the longer market lane.
18. **A Voice Without a Name:** broadcast a partial warning without exposing Sami or the witness.
19. **Paper on the Water:** retrieve a waterproof case from a ferry landing; it contains riverfront property contracts.
20. **The Broker's Charity:** attend a public food distribution and quietly identify Muntasir and Abu Sahl; the mission ends in a staged raid.

### Act V — The City Turns (Missions 21–25)
21. **A City of Roadblocks:** navigate false road closures and reopen a route for clinic supplies.
22. **Free the Depot Workers:** enter the industrial yard and release workers held for refusing to falsify cargo records.
23. **Tawfiq's Debt:** rescue Tawfiq after his impulsive deal with a fence goes wrong; he admits the shortcut in Act III helped the enemy trace the group.
24. **The Ledger Has Two Pages:** steal back the ledger, discover a missing page, and realize Abu Sahl is coordinating with an insider.
25. **Amjad Tells the Truth:** Amjad reveals he unknowingly delivered a sealed package for Muntasir; he helps identify the drop vehicle and earns the team's trust back.

### Act VI — Dawn Belongs to Everyone (Missions 26–30)
26. **Road to the River:** use Bashir's repaired pickup and Idris's route knowledge to break through the final supply blockade.
27. **The Witness Protection Run:** move the Survivor and Sami between safehouses while enemies search the district.
28. **Hold the Central Depot:** survive a multi-wave assault while residents bring water, medical kits and ammunition.
29. **The Man Behind the Manifest:** confront Muntasir and Abu Sahl, recover the missing ledger page and learn the Broker planned to flee by river.
30. **Dawn of Al-Mirfa:** chase the Broker to the riverfront, expose the evidence through Hiba's broadcast and choose to save the trapped workers before pursuing him. The ending shows the neighborhood reopening the clinic and market; the friends remain together, but the city's rebuilding is only beginning.

## Side-story bank (planned optional content)
- **Tea Before Trouble:** help Mariam retrieve a tea cart after a road closure; unlock a safe contact.
- **The Lost Football:** a lighthearted neighborhood search that gives the friends room to feel like real people.
- **Last Minibus Home:** help Idris repair a minibus before nightfall; unlock a fast-travel route.
- **Auntie's Medicine:** deliver a small clinic package without combat; improve witness trust.
- **The Workshop Bet:** Bashir challenges the hero to recover a rare spare part from a scrapyard.
- **Market Day:** protect stalls during a staged theft and expose a decoy crew.
- **The Old Photograph:** find a family photograph in a looted house and reunite it with its owner.
- **A Radio for the Block:** collect parts so Hiba can set up a community radio point.
- **The Missing Water Tank:** trace a stolen rooftop tank and discover an Abu Sahl property scam.
- **One More Match:** a football challenge with Tawfiq and Amjad; friendship dialogue changes after Act V.

## Release and continuity gates
The current runtime must be expanded from 15 to 30 main missions before calling the longer campaign implemented. Mission IDs, map triggers, NPCs, dialogue, task types, enemy waves, pickups and save/restore must match. The story bible alone is not proof of playable content. Full completion still requires Unreal compilation, in-engine playthrough, final character likenesses, sound, polished assets and packaging.
